# Lesson 6: The shopping list in reverse

## Plan from what you have

Most shopping lists start with recipes and end with a basket full of things you already own. Reverse it. Start with your pantry map and your freezer ledger, see what you already have, and plan the week around that first. Then buy only the gaps.

## The four-line plan

Write the week on four lines: base, protein, fresh and sauce. For example: base, rice and roasted squash; protein, a pot of chickpeas and some eggs; fresh, spinach, cucumber and herbs; sauce, tahini, lemon dressing and chilli oil. If you can fill four lines, you can fill the week.

## Buy fresh twice

Buy hardy vegetables at the weekend and the delicate ones midweek. Cabbage, carrots, squash and onions last. Herbs, salad leaves and soft fruit don't. A small midweek top-up costs less than throwing away a bag of slimy leaves on Friday.

## The last ten minutes of Sunday

After the 90-minute Sunday, spend ten minutes writing next week's four-line plan and updating your restock line. Those ten minutes are what make next Sunday start on time instead of starting with a blank page and an empty fridge.
