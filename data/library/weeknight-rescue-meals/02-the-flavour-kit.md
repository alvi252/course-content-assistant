# Lesson 2: Building a flavour kit

## Four things every plate wants

When a plate tastes flat, it is usually missing one of four things: acid, crunch, heat or herb. Acid is lemon, lime or vinegar. Crunch is toasted seeds, nuts, crisp onions or raw vegetables. Heat is chilli in any form. Herb is anything green and fresh. Before you reach for more salt or more sauce, ask which of the four is missing.

## The kit in a box

Keep a small box in the fridge door with your kit: a lemon, a jar of toasted seeds, a bottle of chilli oil and a bunch of herbs wrapped in a damp paper towel. When the kit lives in one place, you use it. When it is spread across the kitchen, you forget it exists.

## Toast seeds once a week

Toast a big batch of seeds on Sunday in a dry pan until they smell nutty, then let them cool and keep them in a jar. A spoonful of toasted seeds on anything turns leftovers into a dish. It is the cheapest upgrade in this whole course.

## Taste, then fix one thing

Taste the plate. Fix one thing. Taste again. Most people fix three things at once and then can't tell which one helped, so next time they have to guess all over again.
