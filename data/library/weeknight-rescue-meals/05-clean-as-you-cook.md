# Lesson 5: Clean as you cook

## The empty-sink rule

Start every cooking session with an empty sink and an empty dishwasher. It sounds small. It is the single habit that has most changed how my students feel about cooking, because the mess never gets ahead of them.

## Wash in the waiting

Every recipe has waiting time: water coming to the boil, onions softening, something in the oven. Use the waiting to wash whatever you've finished with. By the time dinner is ready, the only things left to wash are the plates you eat from.

## One bowl for scraps

Keep one bowl on the counter for peelings and packaging. One trip to the bin at the end, instead of ten trips during cooking, keeps you at your board and keeps the floor clean.

## The two-minute reset

After dinner, set a two-minute timer and put the kitchen back the way you want to find it tomorrow. Two minutes every night beats one miserable hour of scrubbing on Saturday morning.
