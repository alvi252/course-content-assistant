---
title: "One table, two plates"
format: post
---

For years I cooked two dinners most nights. One for me, one for the person who thinks black pepper is spicy.

Now I cook one meal and serve it two ways. Same base, same pot. The finish changes at the table: chilli oil for me, plain for them.

Put the components out separately and let everyone build their own plate. People eat happier when they choose. And you wash half the pans.

Cook once, eat three ways. Or in this case, two.
