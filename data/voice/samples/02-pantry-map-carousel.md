Slide 1: Your kitchen already has a system. It just isn't written down.
Slide 2: Zone 1, the base. Rice, pasta, lentils, tins. What a meal is built on.
Slide 3: Zone 2, flavour. Spices, vinegar, soy, mustard, citrus. What makes it taste like something.
Slide 4: Zone 3, quick. Eggs, bread, yoghurt, cheese. Dinner in five minutes, no cooking.
Slide 5: Flavour goes at eye level. What you can see, you use.
Slide 6: Tonight: open one cupboard and sort it into three. Just one.
