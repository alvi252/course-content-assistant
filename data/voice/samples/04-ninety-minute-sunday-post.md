---
title: "The 90-minute Sunday"
format: post
---

I used to batch cook for four hours. Every Sunday. Then I'd skip it the minute my weekend got busy, and the week fell apart.

Now the timer is the boss. Ninety minutes, oven and hob both working, and when it rings, I stop. Whatever's cooked is my week. Whatever isn't waits.

It turns out a short session you actually do beats a long one you keep skipping. Ninety minutes is enough for a grain, a tray of roasted vegetables, a pot of something and a sauce.

No guilt, just a plan.

This Sunday: set the timer before you take anything out of the fridge.
