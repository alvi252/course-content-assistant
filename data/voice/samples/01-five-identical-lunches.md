---
title: "Stop making five identical lunches"
format: talking_head
---

You made five identical lunches on Sunday. It's Thursday. You're looking at container number four and you'd rather eat the box.

[on-screen text: the five-container trap]

That's not a willpower problem. That's a plan problem. Same plate, five times, nobody wants that.

So don't cook meals on Sunday. Cook components. One grain, one tray of vegetables, one protein, one sauce.

[on-screen text: components, not meals]

Monday it's a bowl. Tuesday it's a wrap. Wednesday it's fried with an egg on top. Same food. Three different dinners.

This Sunday, cook one tray and one pot. That's it. Future you will thank you.
