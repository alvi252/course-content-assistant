---
title: "Four things a flat plate is missing"
format: carousel
---

Slide 1: Dinner tastes flat? Don't add more salt yet.
Slide 2: Acid. Lemon, lime, a splash of vinegar.
Slide 3: Crunch. Toasted seeds, nuts, raw veg.
Slide 4: Heat. Chilli, any kind.
Slide 5: Herb. Anything green and fresh.
Slide 6: Taste, then fix one thing. Then taste again.
