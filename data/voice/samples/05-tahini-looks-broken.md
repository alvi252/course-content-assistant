Your tahini sauce just went thick and lumpy and you think you ruined it.

[on-screen text: you didn't]

Everyone panics here. I did too, the first time. Keep whisking. Add cold water, one spoon at a time.

[on-screen text: one spoon at a time]

And there it is. Smooth. Pourable. It looked broken and it came back.

That's half of cooking, honestly. Make one jar this Sunday and watch it happen.
