format: talking_head

You know why you hate cooking on weeknights? It's not the cooking. It's the pile of pans after.

[on-screen text: empty sink first]

Before you cook anything, empty the sink. Empty the dishwasher. Then start.

While the water boils, wash the board. While the onions soften, wash the knife.

[on-screen text: wash in the waiting]

By the time dinner's ready, the only thing left is your plate. Try it once tonight.
