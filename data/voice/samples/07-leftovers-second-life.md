---
title: "Leftovers get a second life"
format: post
---

A leftover isn't yesterday's dinner. It's an ingredient you already cooked.

That one shift changed how I use my fridge. Leftover rice becomes fried rice. Roast chicken becomes a wrap filling. Last night's stew becomes a pasta sauce.

Before you eat a leftover again, give it a new shape. Ask: can I crisp it, wrap it or loosen it into a soup? One of those is almost always yes.

And if nobody liked it the first time, let it go. Not every dish deserves a sequel.

Tonight: check your leftover shelf and pick one thing to reshape.
